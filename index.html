St Bride's

Specialists in English bows, traders in all fine string bows. Appraisal services available on request.

London & Cambridge

St Bride's was established out of a desire to foreground the best of English bow making at an accessible entry point. We take our name from St Bride's Church on London's Fleet Street, the resting place of Edward Dodd (1705-1810), bowmaker and father of the legendary John Dodd (1752-1839). Despite largely producing unbranded (and therefore unattributable) bows, Edward Dodd is credited as doing a great deal to improve the English bow, laying the groundwork for his son, 'the English Tourte'.

Every bow that passes through our hands is assessed for maker, condition and playing quality before being offered for sale. We work, above all else, with players, and we are always happy to talk through a bow, its individual quality and history before you come to a decision.
